OASIS: command authority for autonomous machine fleets

No machine moves on a forged, modified, stale, revoked or unsafe order, even when a node of the fleet has been captured. Rust no_std, runs on a $1 Cortex-M0+. Proven in the lab on real silicon (TRL 4); looking for field pilots to take it to TRL 5–6.

OASIS is not a safety function. It is not a certified functional-safety component: no PL under ISO 13849-1, no SIL under IEC 62061, and no functional-safety certification of any kind. It reduces no machine risk. What it does is decide whether a received order is authentic, authorised, fresh and within configured limits; risk reduction remains the job of the manufacturer's safety-related control system, and the emergency stop stays a dedicated circuit OASIS cannot reach (ISO 13850:2015 4.1.1.3). ISO 13849-1:2023 explicitly excludes IT security from its scope while noting it can affect safety functions, and points to IEC TS 63074 — that is the gap OASIS occupies: below the safety-related control system, never in its place.

An order is executed only if all seven checks pass: verified envelope, authorised origin, not revoked, not expired, sensor-state lock favourable, within limits, not already executed. The decision is a pure function with three Kani proofs: it acts only if every condition holds, never acts when the sensor-state lock is unfavourable, and is total and deterministic.

§2Problem

Fleets of drones, field robots and remote actuators are run by an operator, but their nodes sit in the open and get lost, stolen or opened. Most low-cost meshes trust a key shared by every node, so one captured node can speak for all of them. Open meshes such as Reticulum are built for anonymity and let relays forward data without checking it. Neither design answers the operator's question: can this order move this machine?

§4Architecture

Actuation gate

Nine-condition decision before any actuator moves, and a stop that keeps only three of them so cryptography can never block it. Bound to the actuator's own clock and boot id, so no shared clock is needed.

Authority

Operator key (single or k-of-n) signs revocation lists, enrollment attestations, ownership transfers and firmware manifests. These rare messages are signed with Ed25519 and ML-DSA-44 (FIPS 204); a per-kind minimum suite is persisted and never lowered, so a downgrade is refused before any signature work. Frequent orders stay Ed25519.

Identity and lifecycle

Key generated on the board; enrollment with proof of possession; ownership transfer needs the old and the new owner; signed A/B firmware update with anti-rollback floor and automatic revert.

Signed envelope (v0B)

Ed25519 over a domain tag, network id, origin, counter, payload length and SHA-256 of the payload. Relay state changes only after full verification.

Transport security

ChaCha20-Poly1305, X25519 and Noise-KK for confidentiality between peers. Standard primitives only.

Link

Wired UART on silicon. LoRa SX1262 driver tested against a mock only. Flooding with TTL; no routing yet.

§8Known limits

  • No key protection and no secure boot on RP2040: a captured node gives up its key over SWD or BOOTSEL, and BOOTSEL can write any image. It can sign only as itself and can be revoked. Planned: RP2350 OTP and secure boot.
  • On-board key generation uses the RP2040 ring oscillator, which is not a validated entropy source; a nonce from the enrollment tool is mixed in once.
  • Post-quantum covers authority messages only: no ML-KEM, frequent orders are Ed25519.
  • Modbus gateway: RTU over a TTL wire, one device, FC06 on silicon; it protects the device only if it is the device's only path.
  • Firmware updates over USB only, not over the mesh; the bootloader is not itself updatable.
  • A revoked node can still relay, delay or drop other nodes' traffic; it cannot modify it.
  • k-of-n operator quorum and relay catch-up after a missed revocation are tested on PC only.
  • Flooding, not routing: every signed message costs one verification per relay.
  • No radio on hardware, no power measurement, no certification, no external audit.
  • The decision journal is tamper-evident against a remote attacker only: whoever holds the flash can rewrite the chain.
  • The radio duty cycle is enforced: the LoRa transport owns its budget and refuses to transmit outside it. But the time-on-air formula is not verified at the source, and no radio has ever transmitted.
  • No node-key rotation, and at most 16 revoked nodes: this is what governs any cryptographic migration.
  • Mesh orders travel in clear: v0B and v0C authenticate, they do not encrypt.
  • Against an insider, the per-link budget caps a relay's CPU but starves legitimate traffic (4/60). It is not a fairness mechanism.
  • Zero customer interviews. Every market hypothesis is untested.

§9Regulatory correspondence

Every text below has its own file in docs/compliance/ with the verbatim requirement, a link to the primary source, the date it applies from, and a requirement → coverage → evidence → gap table. Paywalled standards (IEC, ISO) are marked not verified at the source and say where the information came from.

  • Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, Annex III 1.1.9 and 1.2.1, applicable 20 January 2027 — a chained, persisted log of accepted and refused orders, plus firmware identity. The conformity obligation stays with the machine manufacturer (Art. 8 and 10).
  • Cyber Resilience Act — CycloneDX SBOM, 0 known vulnerabilities across 6 dependency trees, a SECURITY.md with contact and 24 h / 72 h deadlines, signed A/B updates. Updates are USB, not over the network.
  • RED delegated act / EN 18031 — message authentication and a secure update mechanism. No lab assessment, and no radio has ever transmitted.
  • IEC 62443-4-2 — CR 1.2 identity, CR 2.8 auditable log, CR 7.1 denial of service, measured (0.70 ms against 179.4 ms per forged frame), EDR 3.10 update. Boot integrity is partial: no secure boot. The −4−1 development process is open.
  • IEC TS 63074:2023 — the boundary document. It exists to say OASIS is not a safety function: the PL or SIL stays in the certified control system.
  • EU post-quantum roadmap (high-risk cases migrated by end 2030) — authority messages are already hybrid Ed25519 + ML‑DSA‑44 on silicon, which is that milestone's named "quantum-safe firmware updates". A per-hop post-quantum signature does not fit LoRa's legal duty cycle at any spreading factor.
  • MAVLink 2 signing, 9 documented limits — a per-node signed order travels inside a V2_EXTENSION and the gate decides before arming. Nothing on PX4 SITL yet.

None of this is a declaration of conformity. OASIS is not certified, not audited and not assessed by a notified body. The Machinery Regulation creates no downward chain of obligations toward an integrated component, which is why the sellable deliverable is the evidence dossier and not the code — the code is MIT.

For teams running drones, field robots or remote actuators under one operator. Together we take OASIS from TRL 4 to TRL 5–6 on your hardware:
