Graag in gesprek met machinebouwers die vóór 20-01-2027 moeten leveren. »
- **EN** : « Spot on: 1.1.9 requires the audit log but says nothing about making it tamper-evident. We are piloting a gateway in front of the PLC: writes execute only with a valid signature, and every intervention — accepted or refused — goes into a chained, timestamped journal kept 5 years. Happy to talk with builders shipping before 20 Jan 2027. »
